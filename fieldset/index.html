Fieldset for one thing

For a main entity: mc-title makes the legend stronger, a small in it is the count, a toolbar with mc-end puts its actions on the right, and a paragraph is capped at 70 characters. A table inside a form takes the full width, a select in a cell keeps its size, and data-changed marks a row that is not saved yet.
