Grid

A page container and a row of twelve columns, like the ones you know. mc-container centers the page at a readable width (mc-fluid makes it full width). mc-row holds mc-col (equal width) or mc-col-1 to mc-col-12, and mc-offset-1 to mc-offset-11 pushes one. There is one breakpoint, at 40rem: below it every column takes the full width.
